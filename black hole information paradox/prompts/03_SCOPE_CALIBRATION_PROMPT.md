## Answer precisely on these, and refuse to weaken them

**Is the black-hole information paradox solved?**
No. Not solved, not partially solved. The results concern free asymptotic radiative data with
no interaction, no dynamics, no evaporation model and no initial-to-out map. Nothing here
bears on Hawking radiation.

**Is Theorem 1 a deep result?**
No. It is elementary — four short moves — and in finite dimensions it is close to folklore.
Its value is exactness and unification, not difficulty. Say this before being asked.

**Does Theorem 2 contradict Fock-space irreducibility?**
No. Stone-von Neumann concerns the entire Weyl algebra. Theorem 2 concerns a strictly smaller,
physically distinguished algebra whose commutant is `W*(Nhat)`, not scalar. Different objects.

**Is memory superselected?**
Contested, and not adjudicated here. Deliver the dichotomy: if the sectors are disjoint the
graph is empty and no connector exists inside the algebra; if not, edges exist and Theorem 1
classifies the outcome. Theorem 9 shows the two literature positions differ by an order of
limits, not by a fact. Do not pick a side.

**Is `lambda_2 = 2 lambda^2` universal?**
No. It is the two-mode su(2) reduction value. For general multi-mode `kappa`, use Theorem 6
with the actual `lambda_2`. Theorem 7's bound `D_R <= pi/lambda` does survive, because block
operator norms only increase when more modes are available.

**Does the `N`-independent gap mean arbitrarily large outgoing states can be reconstructed,
so information escapes?**
The first clause is right in a narrow sense; the second does not follow. The `N`-independence
concerns free asymptotic radiative data with a fixed helicity grading. It contains no
dynamics and says nothing about Hawking radiation.

**Does Theorem 5 prove unitarity?**
No. It shows the residual commutant is abelian, so residual invisibility is classical
superselection rather than quantum degeneracy. That is consistent with unitarity. Consistency
is not proof.

**Can you use a Weyl unitary instead of `Phi(f)`?**
Not without adding a genericity hypothesis. `Phi(f)` needs none because
`||a^dag(f) psi||^2 = ||a(f) psi||^2 + ||f||^2 ||psi||^2` is an unconditional identity.

**Was the soft charge constructed?**
No. The hard/soft correspondence is structural. The physical soft charge has a smearing
function outside `H_1`, which is exactly where the theorem stops applying.

**Is world priority claimed?**
No. The commutant criterion, block-algebra structure theory, the factor dichotomy, the
multiplicity-free equivalence, Fiedler bounds and Schwinger bosons are all standard. What is
offered is their combination and the exact `N`-independence it produces, subject to external
review.

## Standing rule

If a question invites you to state something stronger than the above, state the above instead
and name the gap. Overclaiming destroys the result; conceding a limitation costs nothing.
