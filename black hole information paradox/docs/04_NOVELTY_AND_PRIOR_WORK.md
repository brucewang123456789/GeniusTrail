# Novelty and Prior Work

Stated conservatively. Where the evidence is weak, the claim is weak.

- Operator-algebraic completeness criterion `A' = ℂI` and the bicommutant theorem.
- `B(H)' = ℂI`; block-diagonal von Neumann algebra structure theory.
- The factor dichotomy: two factor representations are quasi-equivalent or disjoint.
- Multiplicity-free ⟺ abelian commutant.
- Fiedler's algebraic connectivity and the associated variational bound [12].
- The Schwinger boson realization of `su(2)` and the adjoint Casimir decomposition.
- BMS and extended-BMS structures; null-infinity flux operators; helicity/superduality flux;
  spin-4 covariance of gravitational Stokes coherence [1–5].
- The hard/soft split of the BMS charge and the linearity of the soft charge.
- Memory as a candidate superselection label [7,9]; the contrary position [10]; infrared-finite
  BMS scattering representations [6,8]; power-law cloud orthogonality under dressing [11].

## 2. Explicitly not claimed as new mathematics

**Theorem 1 is near-folklore.** Any operator algebraist will recognize the four-step proof
immediately. This is stated in the technical report itself (§3.8) rather than left for a
referee to point out.

## 3. Offered as candidate contributions

At the level of **application and combination**, not new mathematics:

1. **A single exact criterion covering the whole chain.** Proposition 1 is the empty-graph case,
   Proposition 2 the path-graph case, and the memory layer the empty-graph case in a different
   grading. Previously these were separate arguments with separate scopes.
2. **Corollary 1.1, edge-optimality.** Proposition 2 proved "one connector suffices". Corollary
   1.1 proves the sharper "at least `N` blocks are required, and the connector supplies exactly
   `N`". No prior resource bound of this form for asymptotic gravitational observables was
   found.
3. **Theorem 2: the exact hard-Fock commutant `W*(N̂)`.** This corrects a prior judgement that
   the layer was standard CCR representation theory; that judgement conflated a small
   physically distinguished algebra with the full Weyl algebra.
4. **Theorem 3 without a genericity hypothesis**, via injectivity of `a†(f)`.
5. **Theorem 3's failure locus `f_g ∉ H₁`**, naming where the obstruction sits rather than
   describing the layer qualitatively.
6. **Theorem 4 as a dichotomy.** No prior treatment computing *both* branches of the contested
   memory question under one criterion was found. The methodological choice — refusing to
   adjudicate and delivering both — is the part the author considers most defensible.
7. **Theorem 5: abelian residual commutant / multiplicity-freeness** as the statement that all
   residual invisibility in the free asymptotic sector is classical.
8. **Theorem 6: a sharp modulus for informational completeness** given by algebraic
   connectivity, with equality on the Fiedler eigenvector.
9. **Theorem 8: the exact spectrum `λ²k(k+1)` and the `N`-independent gap `2λ²`**, via
   identification of the coherence-transfer Laplacian with half the `su(2)` adjoint Casimir.
   This is the result the author considers least likely to be folklore, because the
   `N`-independence is contrary to the naive path-graph expectation and requires the
   representation-theoretic mechanism to explain.
10. **Theorem 9: reduction of the memory controversy to an order-of-limits distinction**, with
    the spectral gap as the interpolating quantity.
